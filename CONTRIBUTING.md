# Contributing to Second-Hand Tigers

Thank you for helping share experience and shape future engineers. This page explains how contributions are licensed and what to confirm before you submit anything. It applies to every repository in the [second-hand-tigers](https://github.com/second-hand-tigers) organization. The organization-wide policies live on the [.github wiki](https://github.com/second-hand-tigers/.github/wiki).

## How contributions work

Contributors are currently invited by the organization, and all contributors must be adults (18 or older). If you have a correction or suggestion, open an issue in the relevant repository or email second-hand-tigers@googlegroups.com. Please discuss larger changes before writing them.

## License of your contribution

By contributing anything to this repository or any part of it, you agree that the terms below apply. "Anything" includes files, wiki pages, issues and comments, pull requests and reviews, discussions, project board items, release assets, GitHub Pages content, and attachments you upload. You agree that:

1. **Your contribution is licensed under the same terms as the rest of the repository**, as stated in its `LICENSE` file. Unless that file says otherwise, written content is licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) and code is licensed under the [MIT License](https://opensource.org/license/mit).
2. **You keep whatever copyright you hold** in your contribution, and you will not assert separate copyright claims or add legal or technical restrictions that limit what the license allows others to do with the combined work.
3. **These licenses cannot be revoked.** Once your contribution is published and reused, that permission is permanent, so submit only material you are comfortable sharing openly for good.

Some repositories belong to an individual author, such as `<username>-career-learnings`. In those, the repository's own `LICENSE` file and page footers govern. Do not add your pages to another author's repository unless you have agreed to it with them.

## Confirm before you submit

- **It is yours to license.** You wrote it, or you have the right to share it under the license above.
- **You are free to share it publicly.** If it draws on your work experience, make sure it contains no confidential or proprietary information, and get any approval your employer or institution requires.
- **Third-party material is handled properly.**
  - Quote only short excerpts for commentary, with attribution. Do not paste long passages from books, articles, films, songs, or games.
  - Use images only if you created them or they are licensed for reuse.
  - Third-party material stays under its owner's rights, not this license.
- **No one is identifiable.** Do not include names or details that could identify students or other private individuals. See the [Management of Private Information](https://github.com/second-hand-tigers/.github/wiki/Management-of-Private-Information) policy.
- **AI use follows the [AI Use Guidelines](https://github.com/second-hand-tigers/.github/wiki/AI-Use-Guidelines).** Raw AI output stays in Issues, headed with: *AI Generated Overview by [Tool Name] — DO NOT USE VERBATIM, verify information used against citable sources — see AI Use Guidelines.* Published pages carry only material you have verified and rewritten in your own words.
